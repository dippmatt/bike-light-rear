# Stationary is detected by acceleration magnitude near 1g

Smart auto-off requires the acceleration magnitude to stay within a tolerance band around 1g for the stationary timeout (`STATIONARY_TIMEOUT_MS`, defined in time, not in samples), instead of measuring the absence of change. A bike at constant speed on perfectly smooth road would also read 1g, but real roads are uneven enough that a moving bike regularly leaves the band, which resets the count. Detecting "no variation" was the alternative and was rejected. Do not "fix" this to a variance measure without checking how long a real ride stays inside the band. Because the timeout is defined in time, a faster accelerometer polling rate checks more samples within the same period and makes the reset more likely to trigger while riding, not less.
